EAN-13 FAQ

Should I enter 12 or 13 digits for EAN-13?

Enter 12 digits to calculate the final check digit, or enter all 13 digits when you already have a complete valid EAN-13 number.

Can I generate an EAN-13 batch from Excel?

Yes. Select Batch & A4 PDF, import the spreadsheet, and map the product-code column. Invalid rows are reported before output.

What is the difference between EAN-13 and UPC-A?

EAN-13 contains 13 digits and is widely used internationally. UPC-A contains 12 digits and is common in North American retail systems.
