MicroPDF417 vs PDF417

MicroPDF417 is designed for smaller amounts of data and a more compact symbol. Standard PDF417 provides more room for longer records and is usually the safer choice for document, shipping, and larger label workflows. MicroPDF417 is not simply a scaled-down image of a PDF417 barcode, so confirm that the scanner and downstream system support it.

When MicroPDF417 can be useful

  • Short part, item, asset, or document identifiers.
  • Labels where a stacked symbol is required but space is limited.
  • Testing an existing workflow that explicitly specifies MicroPDF417.

Printing and scanning tips

  • Use dark bars on a clean white background.
  • Do not crop the white quiet area around the symbol.
  • Increase scale instead of stretching a downloaded image unevenly.
  • Test the final printed label, not only the on-screen preview.

MicroPDF417 FAQ

How is MicroPDF417 different from PDF417?

MicroPDF417 is designed for smaller spaces but supports less data. Standard PDF417 is better when capacity and flexible symbol dimensions matter more than size.

Can I batch-print MicroPDF417 labels?

Yes. Select Batch & A4 PDF above to create an A4 or Letter sheet from pasted rows, CSV or Excel.

Why does a MicroPDF417 value fail to render?

The value may exceed the capacity available for the compact symbol. Shorten the value or switch to standard PDF417 for more capacity.
